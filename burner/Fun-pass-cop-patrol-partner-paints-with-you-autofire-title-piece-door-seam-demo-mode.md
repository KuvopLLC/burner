## Problem statement

PM feedback: the game isn't fun yet. Color-hunting is tedious; the cop is a binary hide-and-wait loop ('HIDE!' floats over the cop as if he says it); the partner never appears or helps; the title drips don't read as paint and just freeze; the door piece runs to the leaf edge with no rubber seam; NIGHT shows twice on intermission; no attract demo.

## Possible solution

1. Cop patrols with a flashlight vision cone: paint while his back is turned, hide when he stops and scans; '!' when spotted, narrator message instead of floating HIDE!.
2. Partner sprite paints alongside the player (correct colors, own burst cadence, perk-modified) and dives for cover during trouble.
3. Hold X / mouse to autofire bursts; one decoy can (6 total).
4. Title logo rendered by the piece generator with living palette-colored drips (run, bead, fade, respawn).
5. Door leaves: piece inset behind rubber gasket so a seam splits it on open.
6. Intermission stops double-showing NIGHT.
7. Attract demo after 10s idle on title: AI plays a real wall with DEMO + instructions overlay; any key exits.
